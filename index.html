MYSA Registration Agreements

Applies to all children on this registration. Current season: Basketball. Draft — subject to Texas legal and insurance review.

1. Waiver of Liability

As the parent or legal guardian, I give permission for my child(ren) to participate in Muleshoe Youth Sports Association (MYSA) youth sports activities. I understand sports involve risks of injury, including falls, collisions and other accidents. To the fullest extent permitted by Texas law, I release MYSA, its board members, coaches, volunteers and representatives from claims arising from ordinary negligence connected with participation. This release does not cover gross negligence, reckless or intentional misconduct, or liability that cannot legally be waived.

2. Parent/Guardian Code of Conduct

  • I will put children's safety and well-being ahead of winning.
  • I will respect players, coaches, officials, spectators and families.
  • I will respect officials' and coaches' decisions.
  • I will not use profanity, threats, bullying or violence.
  • I will encourage my child and demonstrate good sportsmanship.
  • I will not ridicule anyone at an event or online.
  • I will follow MYSA rules and help keep sports positive for everyone.

3. Parent Participation

Parent involvement is important to MYSA. I understand parents may be asked to assist with the game clock, scorekeeping, concessions, gym cleanup or other volunteer duties during the season.

Preview only: these agreements are not saved or submitted. MYSA should have the final wording reviewed before live use.
